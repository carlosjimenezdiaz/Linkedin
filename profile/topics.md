# Content Pillars & Topic Map

Carlos posts across 4 core pillars. Aim for balance — no single pillar should exceed 40% of monthly posts.

---

## Pillar 1: Agentic AI

**Core angle:** Carlos has hands-on experience building multi-agent systems in production. He writes about what actually works vs. what sounds good in blog posts.

**Seed topics:**
- Multi-agent orchestration patterns (which frameworks hold up at scale)
- LLM memory and context management in long-running agents
- Failure modes of autonomous agents — what breaks and why
- Agentic systems in financial workflows (monitoring, reporting, research)
- Human-in-the-loop design: when to keep humans and when to remove them
- Evaluating agent reliability before deploying to production
- The tooling gap: what still doesn't exist for agentic AI in enterprise

**Hashtags to use:** `#AgenticAI` `#LLMEngineering` `#AIAgents` `#GenerativeAI`

---

## Pillar 2: Traditional ML

**Core angle:** Real ML isn't Kaggle. Carlos writes about the unglamorous but critical parts of building ML systems that survive contact with production.

**Seed topics:**
- Feature engineering as the real competitive advantage (not model architecture)
- Why ML models in finance degrade faster than in other domains
- Data leakage: the silent killer of financial ML backtests
- MLOps for regulated industries — what compliance requires
- When to use a simple model vs. when complexity is justified
- The reproducibility crisis in applied ML
- Model monitoring: what metrics actually matter post-deployment

**Hashtags to use:** `#MachineLearning` `#MLOps` `#DataScience` `#FeatureEngineering`

---

## Pillar 3: Wealth & Asset Management

**Core angle:** Carlos bridges the gap between quant finance and modern AI. He writes for practitioners — PMs, quant analysts, tech leads — not retail investors.

**Seed topics:**
- ML applications in systematic trading strategies
- Alternative data: what's actually alpha and what's noise
- Portfolio optimization beyond mean-variance (and its limits)
- LLMs for financial research and document analysis
- Factor investing + ML: where they converge and where they conflict
- The real costs of live trading infrastructure
- Risk-adjusted performance attribution using ML

**Hashtags to use:** `#QuantFinance` `#AssetManagement` `#AlgoTrading` `#FinancialML`

---

## Pillar 4: Risk Management

**Core angle:** Risk isn't just math — it's organizational. Carlos writes about model risk, where assumptions hide, and how AI changes the risk landscape.

**Seed topics:**
- Model risk management in the era of LLMs
- Stress testing financial models: what regulators want vs. what you need
- The assumptions buried in your risk model (and how to find them)
- AI-driven risk systems: new capabilities, new blind spots
- Tail risk in ML-based trading strategies
- Regulatory frameworks for AI in finance (SR 11-7, DORA, etc.)
- When your risk model becomes the risk

**Hashtags to use:** `#RiskManagement` `#ModelRisk` `#FinancialRisk` `#Regulation`
