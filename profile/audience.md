# Target Audience

Understanding who Carlos is writing for shapes every post — the hook, the depth, the CTA.

---

## Primary Audience: Decision-Makers in AI × Finance

**Who they are:**
- CTOs and VPs of Engineering at fintech, asset management, and hedge fund firms
- Heads of Quant Research or Data Science at buy-side firms
- Tech leads building AI systems in regulated financial environments

**What they care about:**
- Practical, battle-tested insights (not vendor marketing)
- How to move fast without breaking compliance
- What actually works in production (vs. what works in a notebook)
- Identifying people they can trust for specialized consulting work

**What resonates with them:**
- Specific, technical detail that shows you've been in the trenches
- Honest assessment of tools and frameworks (including flaws)
- Clear ROI framing — they think in risk/return terms

**How Carlos earns their trust:**
By writing things they recognize from their own work. When they read a post and think "that's exactly the problem we have" — that's the moment they DM him.

---

## Secondary Audience: Builders & Practitioners

**Who they are:**
- ML engineers and data scientists working at financial firms
- Quant researchers exploring ML approaches
- Senior developers building AI-native financial tools at startups

**What they care about:**
- Technical depth and specificity
- Career growth and specialization
- Learning from someone who's done it

**How they contribute to Carlos's goal:**
They amplify his content (comments, reposts), increasing visibility to the primary audience. They also refer him to their organizations when consulting needs arise.

---

## Tertiary Audience: Founders & Investors

**Who they are:**
- Founders building AI tools for finance (wealth tech, risk tech, trading infrastructure)
- VC/PE professionals tracking the AI × finance space

**What they care about:**
- Market trends and where the puck is going
- Identifying credible technical advisors and consultants

**How Carlos engages them:**
Lighter touch — broad trend posts and research commentary rather than deep technical content.

---

## What Carlos Is NOT Writing For

- Retail investors / personal finance audience
- "Motivational" entrepreneur crowd
- Generic AI enthusiasts with no domain context

Posts that target these groups dilute his SME positioning. Keep the content specialized.

---

## Audience-to-CTA Mapping

| Audience | Best CTA |
|---|---|
| CTOs / VPs | "If you're evaluating [technology] for your firm, happy to share what I've seen. DM me." |
| ML practitioners | "What's your approach to [problem]? Curious how others are solving this." |
| Quant researchers | "Drop a comment — always interested in how others handle this at scale." |
| Founders | "Building in this space? Let's connect — always interested in what practitioners actually need." |
