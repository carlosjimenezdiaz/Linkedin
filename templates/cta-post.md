# Template: CTA Post

**Use when:** Deliberately driving consulting inquiries or positioning for specific types of work. Use sparingly — max 1x per 2 weeks, or it feels like advertising.

**Target length:** 150–220 words

**Goal:** Surface Carlos's availability and specific expertise to people who are ready to hire. Must feel like value delivery, not self-promotion.

---

## Rule: The CTA Post Must Lead With Value

A CTA post that starts with "I'm available for consulting..." is dead on arrival. Every CTA post must lead with a genuine insight, observation, or question. The offer comes last, and it should feel like a natural conclusion.

---

## Structure

```
[HOOK — sharp observation about a problem the right client is experiencing]

[THE PROBLEM IN DETAIL — 2–4 sentences describing the pain point specifically]
  Use language that decision-makers at target firms would recognize.

[WHAT SOLVING IT LOOKS LIKE — 2–3 sentences: what good looks like, or what approach works]
  This demonstrates expertise without giving away everything.

[THE OFFER — 1–2 sentences: specific, low-friction, clear]
  Don't say "I'm a consultant." Say "If you're dealing with [specific thing], let's connect."

[HASHTAGS]
```

---

## Offer Framings (pick the most relevant)

- **The Audit:** "If you want a second pair of eyes on your [model/architecture/approach], I do focused working sessions."
- **The Advisory:** "If you're evaluating [vendors/tools] for [use case] and want a practitioner's read, DM me."
- **The Collaboration:** "Building something in [space] and want someone who's been in production with it? Let's talk."
- **The Short Engagement:** "I take on a small number of short-term advisory engagements each quarter. If your timing matches, let's explore it."
